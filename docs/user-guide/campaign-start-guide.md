# Blessing Tree Campaign Start Guide

This guide is for an app administrator or campaign manager who is setting up a new Blessing Tree campaign. Complete the steps in order. Keep the campaign in **Draft** while you work. Change it to **Active** only after the Readiness screen has no blockers.

## Before You Start

Have these decisions ready:

- [ ] Campaign name and year
- [ ] Campaign start and end dates
- [ ] Campaign purpose or theme
- [ ] One campaign manager and a backup manager
- [ ] Dates for recipient intake, sponsor signup, gift turn-in, and pickup
- [ ] Gift limits for sponsors and recipients
- [ ] The email address and location sponsors will use for gift drop-off
- [ ] A previous campaign to copy, if one already has the setup you want

## Step 1 Create the Campaign

1. Open **Campaigns** from the left menu.
2. Find **Create a Campaign**.
3. Enter the campaign information described below.
4. Select **Create Campaign**.
5. On the new campaign card, select **Make Current**.
6. Select **Open Studio**.

### Campaign Fields

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign Name | Use a name staff will recognize, such as `Blessing Tree 2027`. |
| Year | Enter the year in which the campaign will operate. |
| Status | Choose **Draft** while setting up the campaign. |
| Campaign Purpose | Enter a short theme, such as `Giving and Love` or `Christmas Giving`. This may appear in campaign materials and gift tags. |
| Public Sponsor Slug | Enter a short, unique value using letters, numbers, and hyphens, such as `blessing-tree-2027`. This becomes part of the public sponsor link. Do not use spaces. |
| Enable Public Sponsor Signup | Leave this off until the sponsor dates, gifts, and public page are ready. You can turn it on later in Settings. |
| Start Date | Enter the first day staff will work in this campaign. This is the broad campaign window, not just sponsor signup. |
| End Date | Enter the final day of the campaign, after pickup and closeout work. |
| Start From Previous Campaign | Choose last year's campaign when possible. This copies the roster, teams, communication templates, milestones, and manual schedule events. Review all copied dates and people. |
| Description | Write one or two sentences explaining who the campaign serves and what the team is doing. |

**Recommended choice:** Copy the previous campaign if it was configured correctly. Start from scratch only for a new type of campaign.

## Step 2 Review Settings

1. In Campaign Studio, open **Settings**.
2. Confirm the name, year, purpose, description, and campaign dates.
3. Confirm the public sponsor slug is easy to read.
4. Leave the status as **Draft**.
5. Save any changes.

The start and end dates should cover the entire campaign. Individual activities, such as sponsor signup and gift pickup, are entered later as milestones.

## Step 3 Set Up the Team

1. Open **Team** in Campaign Studio.
2. Confirm that at least one person is a **Campaign Manager**. The person who creates the campaign is added as a campaign manager automatically.
3. Add a second campaign manager as a backup.
4. Add coordinators and volunteers who will help with People, Sponsors, Gifts, or Reports.
5. Give each person only the app access needed for their work.
6. Add people to operational teams when useful, such as Sponsor Intake, People Intake, Gift Receiving, or IT.

**Important:** A team title describes a person's job. App access controls what the person can see and change in Blessing Tree. A person may be listed on the campaign roster without having a login.

## Step 4 Set the Gift Rules

Open **Gift Rules** in Campaign Studio and review every field.

| Setting | Simple starting recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Gifts per Sponsor | `3` is a practical starting limit. Raise it only when sponsors are expected to take more. |
| Wishlist Gifts per Person | `3` works well for most campaigns. |
| Recipient Coverage Rule | Choose **All gifts sponsored** when every requested gift should be covered. Choose **At least one gift sponsored** when one gift is enough to count the person as covered. |
| Required Sponsored Gifts | Use this only with **Minimum number sponsored**. Enter the number that counts as fulfilled. |
| Reservation Hold Minutes | `1440` gives a public sponsor 24 hours to finish a temporary reservation. |
| Allow Partial Sponsor Commitments | Turn this on only if sponsors may select some, but not all, of a person's requested gifts. |

Select **Save Gift Rules** when finished.

## Step 5 Add the Important Dates

1. Open **Schedule**.
2. Add each milestone below by clicking the calendar or a missing date in Calendar Overview.
3. Check that every date falls between the campaign start and end dates.

Add these dates for every campaign:

- Registration Opens
- Registration Closes
- Sponsor Outreach Starts
- Gift Intake Starts
- Gift Intake Ends
- Gift Turn-In Due
- Pickup Window Opens
- Pickup Window Closes
- Campaign Closes

If the public sponsor page will be used, also add:

- Sponsor Registration Starts
- Sponsor Registration Ends

Add at least one manual planning event, such as volunteer orientation, gift sorting, wrapping day, or site setup.

**Date order to check:** recipient registration, sponsor signup, gift intake, gift turn-in deadline, pickup, then campaign close.

## Step 6 Create the Email Templates

1. Open **Communications**.
2. Create the email templates your team will need.
3. Select the correct audience for each template.
4. Add merge fields for names, gift details, dates, links, or the sponsor drop-off QR code.
5. Save the template.
6. Send a test email to yourself.
7. Read the test on both a computer and a phone.

At minimum, create:

- Sponsor welcome or gift commitment confirmation
- Sponsor gift drop-off reminder
- Final sponsor reminder
- Volunteer instructions, if volunteers will receive email from Blessing Tree

Do not send a real campaign email until the test email shows the correct names, dates, gifts, links, and QR code.

## Step 7 Schedule the Messages

1. Return to **Schedule**.
2. Add the communication to the calendar.
3. Choose the saved email template and audience.
4. Choose the send date or connect the message to a milestone.
5. Save the schedule.

Examples include a sponsor reminder before Gift Turn-In Due and volunteer instructions before a receiving or pickup event.

## Step 8 Prepare Public Sponsor Signup

Complete this step only if sponsors will register and choose gifts online.

1. Make sure the public sponsor slug is set in **Settings**.
2. Make sure Sponsor Registration Starts, Sponsor Registration Ends, and Gift Intake Ends have dates.
3. Enter enough recipients and wishlist gifts for sponsors to choose from.
4. Open the public sponsor link and test it yourself.
5. Test the sponsor verification email.
6. Test selecting a gift.
7. Return to **Settings** and turn on **Enable Public Sponsor Signup**.
8. Open the public page one more time to confirm it is available.

## Step 9 Prepare Printed Materials

1. Open the **Flyer Builder** and confirm the public sponsor QR code opens the correct campaign.
2. Open the **Gift Tag Builder** and review the tag design.
3. Confirm the recipient ID is easy to read.
4. Print one sample flyer and one sample gift tag before printing a large batch.

## Step 10 Review Readiness

1. Open **Readiness** in Campaign Studio.
2. Fix every item marked as a blocker.
3. Review warnings and complete the ones that apply to your campaign.
4. Use the action button on a readiness item to open the screen that needs attention.
5. Return to Readiness after each correction.

Typical blockers include missing campaign dates, no campaign manager, missing sponsor signup dates, and missing gift turn-in timing.

## Step 11 Activate the Campaign

Activate the campaign only after setup and testing are complete.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Change Status from **Draft** to **Active**.
3. Save the campaign.
4. Confirm the correct campaign appears in the campaign selector at the top of the screen.
5. Ask another staff member to sign in and confirm that their access is correct.

## Step 12 Begin Campaign Intake

After activation:

1. Add organizations, families, adults, and children under **People**.
2. Add wishlist gifts for each recipient.
3. Add or verify sponsors under **Sponsors**.
4. Use **Gift Search** to commit gifts to sponsors.
5. Use **Dashboard** and **Readiness** to watch for missing setup or operational problems.

## Final Setup Checklist

- [ ] The correct campaign is selected at the top of the screen.
- [ ] Name, year, description, purpose, start date, and end date are correct.
- [ ] At least one campaign manager is assigned; a backup manager is recommended.
- [ ] Staff and volunteers have the correct app access.
- [ ] Gift Rules have been reviewed and saved.
- [ ] All required milestones have dates.
- [ ] At least one manual planning event is on the calendar.
- [ ] Email templates are active and have been tested.
- [ ] Scheduled messages use the correct template, audience, and date.
- [ ] Public sponsor signup has been tested before it is enabled.
- [ ] Flyer and gift tag samples have been printed and checked.
- [ ] Readiness has no blockers.
- [ ] The campaign is changed to Active only after all checks are complete.

## If Something Looks Wrong

- If a screen is empty, first confirm the correct campaign is selected at the top.
- If a button is missing, ask an app administrator to check your campaign access.
- If public sponsor signup does not open, check the slug, enable setting, and sponsor registration dates.
- If an email does not look right, send another test and check its merge fields.
- If you are unsure what is missing, open **Readiness** or ask **Ask Blessing Tree** how to finish campaign setup.
